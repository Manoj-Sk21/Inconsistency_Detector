Scores & Legend — What the numbers mean
Severity (shown as High / Medium / Low) — internal importance score:
  • High (3): Critical contradictions that can change the investigation (e.g., time mismatch or vehicle/plate mismatch).
  • Medium (2): Important but less decisive (e.g., person/appearance mismatches).
  • Low (1): Missing or minor details (e.g., a keyword only mentioned in one source).
Confidence — how sure the system is about a claim (displayed as a percentage in explanations).
Values close to 100% mean the AI is more confident. Values around 50% mean uncertain — re-check evidence manually.
Similarity score (0.00 → 1.00) — measures overall agreement between two sources (sentence embeddings).
• ≥ 0.75 = strong agreement
• 0.50–0.75 = moderate agreement
• < 0.50 = low agreement / likely different descriptions
What the system outputs
• Top 3 findings — the highest-severity issues to check first.
• Explanation — plain-language description with confidence values.
• Conflicts — structured, machine-readable mismatch objects (used by analysts).
• Similarity Scores — pairwise agreement measures between sources.
